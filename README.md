# Portfolio

Personal site of Leo Lloyd Catalan: a law student at Silliman University and computer science graduate
who builds local-first AI tools for legal study.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish it with GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy from `main`).

## Projects

- **[F5 Studio](https://github.com/lloydonix/f5-studio)**: an offline Mac app that turns long scripts into audio with F5-TTS voice cloning.
- **The Cogitator** (private): a study machine on Open WebUI, grounded in my Obsidian law notes and trusted Philippine legal sources.
- **Scientia Juria Mentor** (private): a law tutor that answers only from my notes, cites every claim, and refuses rather than guesses.
- **Vi** (private): a local AI companion with long-term memory.
- **The Council** (private): several mentor characters in one local chat.
