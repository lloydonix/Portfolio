F5 Studio

An offline Mac app that turns long scripts into audio with a cloned voice.

PythonSwiftF5-TTS

An offline Mac app that turns long scripts, such as study reviewers, lecture notes and tutoring scripts, into long-form audio with F5-TTS voice cloning. You pick a 5–10 second reference voice and it generates audio one paragraph at a time on the Apple GPU.

  • The generation queue survives restarts. Clips are keyed by their text, so editing one line regenerates only that line.
  • A shared pronunciation list handles Latin terms, names and acronyms.
  • Reference voices are trimmed, resampled and transcribed with Whisper. Lines with an unusual pace are flagged for a listen.
  • A fact check flags numbers or citations missing from the source. Export is one merged MP3.
  • A native Swift wrapper around a local Python server. Nothing leaves the Mac.

The Cogitator

A study machine that answers from my Obsidian law notes first, with sources.

PythonOpen WebUIRAG

Open WebUI on a custom OpenAI-compatible bridge. Every question is answered from my Obsidian notes first, with the sources shown as links back into the vault.

  • /digest writes case digests in my own Case Brief template from my notes plus LawPhil, the SC E-Library and the Official Gazette, and refuses rather than digest from memory. /recit runs recitation drills.
  • A PH Law Search toggle limits web lookups to trusted Philippine legal sources.
  • Quiz me and Save as Brief buttons. Saving drafts a new note into the right subject folder and never overwrites one.
  • Scriptorium writes tutor scripts from a reviewer and checks the numbers against the sources, ready for F5 Studio.
  • Groq by default, Mistral as backup, a local qwen3 model offline. The note search always stays on the Mac.

Scientia Juria Mentor

A law tutor that only answers from my notes and cites every claim.

PythonRAGOllama

A strictly grounded tutor for my Obsidian vault. It answers only from my notes (and trusted legal sites when I allow it), cites every claim, and refuses rather than guesses. Its persona is Sakura Mina, a fictional law-school rival I created.

  • Retrieval, then a draft, then a check of every claim against its citation. Unsupported claims are dropped, and a debug view shows why.
  • The vault is opened read-only, and notes-only mode works fully offline.
  • An evaluation harness compares chat models side by side.
  • Its retrieval and claim checks also power the Cogitator.

Vi

A private, local AI companion with long-term memory.

PythonOllamalaunchd

A local AI companion with long-term memory. The chat, the model and every memory stay on the Mac. It's the largest of these projects, with nearly a hundred commits in two weeks.

  • Runs as launchd services that start at login and restart if they crash.
  • Prompts are ordered so Ollama can reuse cached work, bringing replies to about 5–10 seconds on 16 GB.
  • An optional discoveries feed reads a fixed list of public feeds through a safety pipeline and sends nothing out.

The Council

Four mentor characters who talk with you in one chat.

PythonOllamaMulti-agent

Mentor characters inspired by Arthur Morgan, Joshua Graham, Musashi from Vagabond, and Arthas as the devil's advocate. The members with something to say reply, all written in one model pass and shown as separate colored bubbles.

  • Adding a member takes one persona file and one config entry.
  • It shares a single loaded model with Vi, with coordination code so neither app pushes the model out while the other is using it.
